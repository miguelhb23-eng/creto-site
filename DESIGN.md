# Design System: CRETO Ingeniería y Construcciones

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Concrete Ledger"**

This is a corporate builder site at full scale: a concrete-black ground, warm off-white type, and the brand red used as whole fields rather than as sprinkled accents. The professional benchmark is conconcreto.com. It plays the category standard straight and with full craft, and it refuses the light SaaS template: no pill badges, no eyebrow kickers, no card grids of icon plus title. Hierarchy comes from scale. Huge condensed uppercase display lines carry the claims, a quiet humanist grotesk carries the explanations, and thin hairline rules divide everything. There are no boxes or shadows.

The red is reserved for moments of commitment: the budget promise band, the closing call-to-action band, the primary "Cotiza por WhatsApp" action, the sweep behind a service row on hover, the progress rule on each process step, and the active underline in navigation. Everything else stays in three tones of black and three tones of off-white. Imagery runs full-bleed, edge to edge, under dark gradients; on the red closing band it is instead multiplied into the red on the right side. Until real photographs exist, each photo slot draws a technical line drawing (elevation, section, floor plan or hillside retaining wall) that traces itself onto the screen. These drawings are honest stand-ins and must not be read as fake photos.

Motion is confident and slow: long out-expo easings, masked line reveals on page load, blocks that rise into view, and one signature interaction, the budget promise lighting up word by word as you scroll across the red field. All content is visible without JavaScript and under reduced motion.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark ground (`ink-0`) with three tonal steps for surfaces; no shadows except on the floating WhatsApp button.
- Brand red appears as whole fields and full-height sweeps, never as decorative tints.
- Archivo condensed (width 78%) at weight 800, uppercase for display and hero; Hanken Grotesk for all text.
- Hairline rules (`line`, `line-strong`) structure lists, rows, steps and footers instead of cards.
- Corners are almost square (4px); the only round element is the WhatsApp float.
- Line-drawing placeholders that trace on reveal, swapped for photos by editing `src/data/site.ts`.

## Colors

The palette is concrete blacks and warm paper whites, with a single saturated signal red (brand commitment: red, black and white).

### Primary
- **Signal Red** (`red`): The brand red, used for the promise band and closing CTA band backgrounds, primary buttons, the service-row hover sweep, process-step progress rules, nav and filter underlines, the WhatsApp channel icon tile, the WhatsApp float, text selection, the focus outline, caret and form accent color. White on Signal Red measures 5.07:1 (AA for all text sizes).
- **Lit Red** (`red-hover`): The hover state for red buttons and the WhatsApp float. White on Lit Red measures 4.23:1, which passes AA only for large text. It is a transient hover state, never a resting text background.

### Neutral
- **Concrete Black** (`ink-0`): The page ground, the header when scrolled (at 86% opacity with blur), the mobile menu, the footer, and input fields. It also sets `theme-color`.
- **Shadow Concrete** (`ink-1`): The first raised tone, used for `section--ink` bands, the form panel, and the media background behind images and drawings.
- **Form Concrete** (`ink-2`): The neutral notice (`aviso`) background.
- **Cured Concrete** (`ink-3`): The scrollbar thumb only (1.30:1 on `ink-0`); never used for text.
- **Hairline** (`line`): Off-white at 14% opacity. It draws every structural divider: section tops, list rows, footer bands and the header's bottom edge.
- **Strong Hairline** (`line-strong`): Off-white at 28% opacity, for ghost button borders, input borders, the menu button, link-arrow underlines, process-step top rules and the example tag border.
- **Warm Paper** (`text`): The primary text and headlines. It measures 17.73:1 on `ink-0`.
- **Dust** (`text-2`): Secondary text such as leads, body copy, nav links at rest and card meta. It measures 10.24:1 on `ink-0`.
- **Ash** (`text-3`): Tertiary text such as notes, form hints, placeholders, small labels, inactive filters, the muted "CRETO" brand word in service names, and the oversized process numerals (01–04). It measures 6.24:1 on `ink-0` and 5.47:1 on `ink-2`.
- **Pure White** (`on-red`, CSS `--on-red-2`): All text on red fields, primary and secondary alike (promise items, closing lead and note, success notices, service-row text under the sweep), plus the white button. Secondary text on red is solid white, never a translucent white.
- **Alert Pink** (`error`): The border of a `:user-invalid` input (7.13:1 on `ink-0`).

### Named Rules
**The Whole-Field Rule.** Red is applied as a complete surface: a full section band (promise, closing CTA), a full button, a full-height row sweep, a 2px rule at full width. Red text on black, red-tinted cards and red gradients are not part of the system.

**The Three-Tone Ground Rule.** Depth comes from moving one step along `ink-0` → `ink-1` → `ink-2`, never from shadows or lighter greys.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable (with Archivo, system-ui, sans-serif), self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable/archivo/wdth.css`
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk Variable (with Hanken Grotesk, system-ui, sans-serif), self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable/hanken-grotesk/wght.css`

**Character:** A heavy, condensed engineering grotesk does the shouting, and a calm, open humanist grotesk explains in plain Spanish. The condensing comes from `font-stretch` on Archivo's width axis: 78% for headlines and 85% for small titles. There is no separate condensed family.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, `clamp(2.3rem, 10vw, 6rem)`, line-height 0.94, -0.02em, uppercase, stretch 78%): Used only for the home hero headline, set as two masked lines with `max-width: 18ch`.
- **Headline Large / `.h1`** (800, `clamp(2.3rem, 7vw, 5.5rem)`, 0.94, uppercase): Used for interior page heroes, the project detail title and the 404 page.
- **Headline / `.h2`** (800, `clamp(2.3rem, 4.8vw, 4.25rem)`, 0.94, sentence case): Section headings. On the service pages it carries the brand word "CRETO" as a block line above the name (0.42em, uppercase, 0.04em tracking, `text-3`).
- **Large signature sizes** in the same 78%/800/uppercase voice: the promise text (`clamp(2.6rem, 7.4vw, 6rem)`, 0.95, max 16ch), the closing CTA title (`clamp(2.8rem, 7vw, 6rem)`, max 13ch), service row names (`clamp(2rem, 4.4vw, 3.75rem)`), mobile menu links (`clamp(2.4rem, 11vw, 3.5rem)`) and process numerals (4rem, tabular, `text-3`).
- **Title / `.h3`** (800, `clamp(1.6rem, 2.6vw, 2.25rem)`, 1.0): Tertiary headings. Project card titles use 78% at `clamp(1.75rem, 3vw, 2.75rem)`, -0.015em, max 22ch, and a "Tipo · Lugar" title breaks onto two lines (the " · " becomes a line break).
- **Title Small** (750, 1.5rem–1.875rem, 1.1, stretch 85%, sentence case): Promise items, process step titles, method items (1.625rem), project prose headings (1.75rem), the form title (1.875rem) and spec-sheet values (1.375rem, tabular).
- **Lead** (400, `clamp(1.125rem, 1.5vw, 1.3125rem)`, 1.55, `text-2`, max 38rem): The intro paragraph under every headline.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, or 1rem under 600px, 1.6, `text-2`, max 62ch): Running text. Paragraphs and list items use `text-wrap: pretty`, and headings use `balance`.
- **Label** (650, 1rem, 0.005em): Buttons, link-arrows and card links. Nav links are 550 at 0.9688rem and form labels are 600 at 0.9375rem.
- **Small** (0.9375rem, and 0.875rem/0.8125rem for meta): Notes, card meta, footer lists and the hero foot line.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Voice Rule.** Archivo is always condensed (78% or 85%) and heavy (750–800). Hanken Grotesk is always the reading voice. Archivo is never set at regular width or weight, and Hanken Grotesk is never used for display.

**The No-Kicker Rule.** Headings stand alone. There are no small uppercase eyebrow labels above them. The only brand-word device is "CRETO" inside service names (inline and muted in rows, a block line on service pages), and it is part of the name itself.

## Layout

- **Container:** max 1360px (`container`), centered, with a fluid gutter (`clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 3rem)`).
- **Section rhythm:** vertical padding of `clamp(5rem, 11vw, 9.5rem)`. Larger moments get more air: the promise band uses `clamp(6rem, 13vw, 11rem)` and the closing CTA uses `clamp(6rem, 12vw, 10rem)`. Service-page sections use `clamp(4rem, 9vw, 7.5rem)`. Adjacent sections are separated by a top hairline (`section--line`) or by a tone shift to `ink-1` (`section--ink`).
- **Section head:** a two-column grid (1.1fr / 1fr, gap 1.5rem × 4rem, bottom-aligned) with the headline left and the lead right, followed by `clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5rem)` of space. `head--solo` is a single column with a max width of 52rem.
- **Hero:** full viewport (`max(640px, 100svh)`; interior pages use `max(460px, 58svh)`), with content anchored bottom-left under a transparent fixed header (76px, or 68px under 900px).
- **Grids:** split 1fr/1fr (gap `clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 6rem)`, media 4:5, or 4:3 when wide or on mobile); a 12-column project grid (cards span 12 / 6 / 4); three columns for promise and method items; four for process steps; five for the project spec sheet; a 1:2 grid for project prose; a 3-column gallery whose first image spans 2×2; and a 0.9fr/1.1fr contact grid.
- **Breakpoints:** at 1080px the four-column grids drop to two and the three-column footer to two columns. At 900px the nav collapses to the full-screen menu, splits, heads, contact and prose stack, service rows become one column, and all cards go full width. At 600px most grids go to a single column (the footer stays two columns, with the brand block spanning both), hero and closing buttons go full width, and the footer bottom and hero foot get extra right or bottom padding so the WhatsApp float never covers content.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from tonal layering on the three blacks, from hairline rules, and from dark gradients laid over full-bleed media. The hero uses a bottom-up plus left-to-right ink gradient, and project cards use a bottom-up gradient (97% at 0, 86% at 32%, 25% at 62%, clear by 80%). The closing CTA has no gradient: it is a red field, and its media is multiplied into the red at 55% opacity. The scrolled header is the one translucent surface (86% `ink-0`, `saturate(140%) blur(14px)`).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Float lift** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`): Used only on the WhatsApp float button.
- **Focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(216, 30, 44, 0.28)`): The input focus state, paired with a red border.

### Named Rules
**The Gradient-Not-Shadow Rule.** Legibility over imagery comes from ink gradients (or, on red, from a multiplied image kept to one side), never from boxes, shadows or text-shadows.

## Shapes

Corners are close to square. The shared radius is 4px (`rounded.md`) for buttons, inputs, the form panel, media frames, project cards, notices, contact icon tiles and the menu button. The example tag and the focus outline use 2px. The only circle is the WhatsApp float. Lines are drawn square-capped (`stroke-linecap: square`) in the technical drawings. The active mobile menu item gets a small red square (0.35em) and no dot. Borders are always 1px hairlines; 2px appears only as red progress or underline rules.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid, compact and sure of themselves.
- **Shape:** gently squared (4px). Heights are 3.25rem by default, 2.75rem for `sm` and 3.75rem for `lg`. Each button is an inline-flex row with an icon gap of 0.6rem, Hanken Grotesk 650, and no wrapping.
- **Primary (red):** red fill with white text. It is used for every "Cotiza por WhatsApp" action and almost always carries the WhatsApp icon.
- **Ghost:** transparent with a `line-strong` border and `text` color. On hover the border turns to `text` and the fill to 6% off-white. It is used for the secondary "Dejar mis datos" action.
- **White:** white fill with `ink-0` text, hovering to `text`.
- **States:** a 0.25s `ease-out` transition on color, border and transform; `:active` nudges down 1px; disabled is 55% opacity. Focus everywhere is a 2px red outline at a 3px offset.

### Link Arrow
A text link with a trailing arrow icon, weight 650, and a 1px `line-strong` underline at 0.25rem offset. On hover the underline turns red and the gap widens from 0.5rem to 0.8rem. It pairs with the primary button as the secondary hero action ("Ver proyectos").

### Navigation (header and menu)
- **Header:** fixed and transparent over the hero. After 24px of scroll (or when the menu is open) it becomes 86% `ink-0` with a blur and a hairline bottom edge. It holds the logo (`logo-light.svg`, 48px high, or 42px on mobile), nav links on the right (gap 2.25rem), and a small red "Cotizar" button.
- **Nav links:** `text-2` at rest, `text` when hovered or active, with a 2px red underline that scales in from the left (0.45s).
- **Mobile menu (under 900px):** a full-screen `ink-0` panel below the header, revealed by a `clip-path` wipe from the top (0.5s `ease-in-out`). Links are huge condensed uppercase lines divided by hairlines, and the active one carries a red square. It ends with a large red WhatsApp button and the response-time note. Escape closes it, scrolling is locked, and the float is hidden while it is open.

### Hero
Full-bleed media (a photo, or the animated line drawing anchored right with `xMaxYMid slice`) sits under a two-way ink gradient. Contents in order: the headline in masked lines, a one-line lead, actions (a large red WhatsApp button and a link-arrow), and a foot line above a hairline (location on the left, response time on the right). The photo settles from a 1.06 to 1.0 scale over 2.4s. Interior heroes are shorter (`max(460px, 58svh)`) and use `.h1` and an optional "Volver" back link (with the example tag beside it on example projects).

### Split
Text and media side by side. The `split--rev` variant swaps them on desktop. Media is 4:5 (or 4:3), 4px radius, and reveals with a slight delay.

### Service Rows (signature)
Big rows separated by hairlines rather than cards. Each row has three columns: the condensed uppercase name rendered inline as "CRETO NAME" with the brand word muted in `text-3`, a summary in `text-2`, and an outlined "Cotizar" WhatsApp chip. The whole row links to its service section through an overlaid link. On hover or `focus-within`, a red field sweeps up from the bottom (`scaleY`, 0.55s), bleeding one gutter beyond the container on both sides. The text and the brand word turn white and the chip flips to solid white with `ink-0` text.

### Promise Field (signature)
A full-width red section. It contains the promise sentence in giant condensed uppercase (max 16ch), followed by three items, each topped by a 45%-white hairline: a title-small heading and white body text.

### Process Steps
A numbered list in four columns. Each step has a 1px `line-strong` top rule, over which a 2px red rule grows to full width as the step reveals (1.2s, staggered). It shows a 4rem tabular numeral (`01`–`04`, CSS counter, `text-3`, legible at 6.24:1), a title-small heading and body text.

### Project Cards
Full-bleed image cards with a 4px radius and `min-height: clamp(380px, 42vw, 560px)`. The big variant spans 12 columns at up to 680px. The info sits bottom-left over a bottom-up gradient (97% → 86% at 32% → 25% at 62% → 0 at 80%): a meta line (category · place · year, plus the `Ejemplo` tag when the project is an example), a condensed title split onto two lines ("Tipo" / "Lugar"), and a "Ver proyecto" link. On hover the media scales to 1.035 (1.2s) and the link widens with a red underline.

### Example Tag
A small outlined tag (1px `line-strong`, 2px radius, 0.8125rem, `text-2`). It is required on every example project, in cards ("Ejemplo") and in the project hero ("Proyecto de ejemplo").

### Filters (portfolio)
A row of text buttons over a hairline, not chips. They are `text-3` at rest and `text` on hover or when active, and the active one gets a 2px red underline scaling in. They use `aria-pressed`.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `ink-0` fill inside the `ink-1` form panel, a 1px `line-strong` border, 4px radius, padding 0.85rem × 1rem, and `text-3` placeholders. Selects use a custom chevron in `text-2`. Labels are 600 weight, and optional hints use 450 weight in `text-3`.
- **Focus:** the border turns red and a 3px red halo at 28% appears. The default outline is suppressed in favor of the halo.
- **Error:** a `:user-invalid` input gets an `error` border.
- **Notices:** neutral notices are `ink-2` with a `line-strong` border. Success notices are solid red with a 60%-white border and white text.

### Contact Channels
Rows divided by hairlines. Each has a 3rem square icon tile (`line-strong` border, or solid red for WhatsApp), a small label and a bold value. Linked rows indent 0.5rem on hover.

### Closing CTA
A reusable section (`ctaBannerHTML`) that is a full red field with white text and no gradient. The media (the hillside drawing until a photo exists) occupies the right 46% of the band with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` at 55% opacity; on the drawing, the main and soft lines turn dark ink (`rgba(11, 11, 10, 0.55)`) and the accent line turns white. It holds a giant condensed title (max 13ch), a solid white lead, a large white "Cotiza por WhatsApp" button (`ink-0` text, hovering to `text`), a large ghost "Dejar mis datos" button with a 70%-white border (hover: solid white border, 10% white fill), and the response-time note in white.

### Footer
A hairline-topped `ink-0` band with no claim headline above it, holding a three-column grid (1.4fr / 0.8fr / 1.2fr): the brand (logo 64px plus the legal name), site links and contact links. Below that sits a hairline bottom bar in `text-3` at 0.875rem. Links go from `text-2` to `text` on hover.

### WhatsApp Float
A fixed 3.5rem red circle in the bottom-right corner (offset `clamp(1rem, 2.5vw, 1.75rem)`), with the only drop shadow in the system. On hover it scales to 1.06 and turns `red-hover`. It is hidden while the mobile menu is open, and it is not shown on the contact page (`body.page-contacto`), where WhatsApp is already the first channel.

### Media Placeholders (technical line drawings)
`mediaHTML()` renders a real `<img>` (lazy, or `fetchpriority="high"` for heroes) when a photo path is set and does not contain "placeholder". Otherwise it renders an inline SVG drawing (`edificio`, `casa`, `planta` or `ladera`) on a radial `ink` gradient, labelled with `role="img"` and the alt text. The drawings use three stroke weights: the main line (42% off-white, 2px), the soft line (20%, 1.25px, for dimensions, floors and context) and a single red line (2.5px) marking one key element: a roofline, a cornice, a door swing or a retaining wall. Strokes are non-scaling and square-capped.

### Motion
- **Easings:** `ease-out` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for nearly everything, and `ease-in-out` `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for the menu wipe.
- **Block reveal:** `[data-reveal]` elements start at opacity 0 and 28px below their position, then rise over 1s (opacity) and 1.1s (transform) when `IntersectionObserver` fires (threshold 0.12, bottom margin -8%). Staggers come from the `--d` delay, in steps of 70–180ms.
- **Hero line masks:** each `.line` clips its inner span, which slides up from 105% (1.15s, delay 0.15s + i × 0.12s). Supporting `[data-intro]` items fade up 16px (1s, delay 0.55s + i × 0.1s).
- **Line-drawing trace:** strokes animate their dash offset from 3000 to 0 over 2.6s when the drawing becomes visible. Soft lines start 0.35s later, and the red line starts 0.9s later and runs 1.6s.
- **Promise illumination:** the promise sentence is split into words that start at 42% white. As the block travels from 92% of the viewport to its center at 45%, words switch to full white in reading order (0.5s color transition, scroll tied through `requestAnimationFrame`). The original sentence stays available as an `aria-label`.
- **No-JS:** every animated start state is scoped to `html.js`, which an inline script in each HTML `<head>` adds before first paint (`initLayout()` adds it again as a fallback), so the site renders fully visible without JavaScript, and the process rules are drawn full.
- **Reduced motion:** all animations and transitions collapse to 0.01ms with no delay, reveals and lines show at their final state, drawings render fully traced, word splitting is skipped, and smooth scrolling is off.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use `red` as a whole surface (a band, a button, a full-height sweep or a 2px full-width rule) and keep it for commitment moments and the WhatsApp action.
- **Do** set display type in Archivo at `font-stretch: 78%`, weight 800, line-height 0.94, -0.02em tracking, uppercase for hero, closing and promise lines, and balance-wrapped with a `ch` max width.
- **Do** divide content with 1px hairlines (`line`, `line-strong`) and tone shifts (`ink-0` to `ink-1`) instead of cards and shadows.
- **Do** run imagery full-bleed under ink gradients (on the red closing band, multiplied on the right instead), and route every photo through `mediaHTML()` so empty slots fall back to the traced line drawing.
- **Do** add real photos by copying them into `public/fotos/` and writing their paths in the `fotos` object in `src/data/site.ts` (horizontal JPG, about 2400px wide).
- **Do** mark every example project with `ejemplo: true` so it shows the `Ejemplo` / `Proyecto de ejemplo` tag.
- **Do** scope animation start states under `html.js` and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** keep a 4px radius on interactive and media elements and a 2px red focus outline at a 3px offset.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put eyebrow kickers or small uppercase labels above headings.
- **Don't** use pill badges or grids of icon plus title cards; the light SaaS template is the confirmed anti-reference.
- **Don't** show invented testimonials, reviews, projects or team data as real, and don't add new figures or claims beyond those already on the site.
- **Don't** show certifications or credentials (no credential strips, credential lists or "respaldado por" columns) unless the client re-approves them.
- **Don't** use red as text on black, as a tint or as a gradient.
- **Don't** add drop shadows; the WhatsApp float is the single exception.
- **Don't** set Archivo at normal width or regular weight, or use Hanken Grotesk for display.
- **Don't** put resting text on `red-hover`; white on it is 4.23:1 and is only used for hover.
